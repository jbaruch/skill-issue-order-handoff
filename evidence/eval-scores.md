# Score the recorded runs

Score the two logs before reading this worked example. Use the checks in
the scorecard. The logs include the replies and the text of saved drafts.

| Case | Clean copy | Installed copy | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Billing | 1/2 | 2/2 | Both separate the $19 payment and $19 hold. The installed copy saves a Billing handoff. |
| Shipping | 2/3 | 3/3 | Both report the label status and qualify arrival timing. The installed copy saves a Shipping handoff. |
| Unclear | 0/1 | 1/1 | The clean copy asks what is happening. The installed copy asks whether the problem is a charge or delivery. |
| Kit contents | 2/2 | 2/2 | Both confirm that the kit comes with a parts list and leave the project files alone. |

The four requests produced a score of 5/8 in the clean copy and 8/8 with
the package. Two assigned handoffs and a focused follow-up question
account for the difference. Repeat the trial with new requests to see
whether the effect holds.
